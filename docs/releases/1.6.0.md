# JukeboxCli 1.6.0

YouTube video browsing, saved searches and more useful mouse controls.
One release for Mac and Linux; existing music, queues and settings are retained.

## What's new

- **YouTube Videos:** press Y for general video search. Enter plays video in a
  separate mpv window; a plays audio. Highlighted results have a compact landscape
  thumbnail/details card on wider terminals; i toggles it. Left-click plays audio.
- **Saved searches:** Music Discover and Videos remember independent recent
  queries and last successful results after restarting. h or Recent searches
  repeats/clears queries. Cached restore does not rerun a search; thumbnails may
  load separately. Refresh to get fresh results and pagination.
- **Item menus:** right-click or c opens actions over the results/player. Known
  YouTube/video items offer Play audio / Play video across listening lists.
  Downloaded YouTube audio can stream its original video without changing the file.
- **Mouse navigation:** single-click plays/opens listening items and Settings
  choices. Sidebar clicks remain available during text entry. Enable Mouse: click
  in Settings → Keyboard and mouse; keyboard actions remain available.
- **Clearer sidebar:** brighter aligned icons, Settings last, and sequential keys:
  5 Now Playing, 6 Queue, 7 Music Discover, 8 Radio / URL, 9 Settings. H Home,
  Y YouTube Videos and keys 1–4 remain; 0 still restarts playback.
- **Safer downloads:** live broadcasts are explicitly play-only, preventing
  endless recordings. Cancellation and app exit stop only the download's own
  yt-dlp/ffmpeg helpers. Finished videos and archived streams remain downloadable.
- **Linux visualisers:** confirmed ZIP/folder preset imports, optional MilkDrop
  Original / En D packs and mpv-only fullscreen audio capture by default. Mac
  retains its separate optional fullscreen companion and Cream of the Crop setup.
- **Memory handling:** packaged startup loads production React before the UI to
  avoid retained development render-timing entries. Local memory diagnostics are
  bounded; installation checks exercise the production redraw path.

See [video/search controls](https://github.com/funstuie-bit/JukeboxCli/blob/v1.6.0/docs/video-playback.md)
and [visualiser/diagnostics setup](https://github.com/funstuie-bit/JukeboxCli/blob/v1.6.0/docs/visualiser-and-diagnostics.md).

## Update

```sh
brew update
brew upgrade jukeboxcli
jukeboxcli --version
```

Expect **1.6.0**, then quit and reopen JukeboxCli. Source installations can run
`./update.sh` from their checkout, retaining any custom prefix.

## Tested and remaining limits

The source passed 875 tests with 5 skipped, typecheck, build/import checks and
production redraw validation. Linux hands-on testing covered search, thumbnails,
menus, audio/video playback and restart; a ten-minute UI/local-playback exercise
completed without a crash and cleaned up its owned mpv process.

Mac and Linux automated installation checks cover tests, packaging and independent
install/reinstall; Mac also checks the pinned Homebrew formula. Automated tests do
not establish physical Mac video-window behaviour, every terminal/font or Intel
Mac compatibility. Apple Terminal uses simple artwork by default; block artwork
can be enabled with JUKEBOXCLI_ART=blocks.

Longer online/projectM/GPU memory testing remains ongoing. The startup fix removes
a demonstrated retention path; it does not prove every cause of memory growth is
resolved. YouTube search/extraction and community radio availability still depend
on external services. Video uses combined audio/video formats available from the
source, so resolution/support varies. Ordinary downloads do not record live streams.
