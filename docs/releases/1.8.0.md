# JukeboxCli 1.8.0

Optional setup is easier to find, and Home has a bit more life. One release for
Mac and Linux. Your existing mouse, cookie and appearance preferences are kept.

## Make it yours

Press **u on Home**, including on first launch, for Setup & extras. Choose mouse
clicks, wheel-only scrolling or keyboard-only controls. Shift-drag selects text
in click mode. Artwork colours and decorative motion toggle in place; themes,
cookies and optional fullscreen effects open their exact Settings pages.

The Home checklist can be dismissed. It stays dismissed after restarting, but
u always brings setup back. Nothing here is required to listen, and first-run
Enter still goes straight to online search.

## Home visuals

Roomy layouts show cover-led recent listening, station tiles and a larger cover
for current/recent music. Home shares Player's optional artwork accents and
respects custom appearance/high contrast. Missing art and Apple Terminal use
text fallbacks; smaller windows keep compact choices.

With decorative motion enabled, the jukebox grille lights move slowly while
music plays. This is decoration, not an audio meter. Reduced motion remains on
by default; pause, loading, help and leaving the view stop the animation.
Home never searches, plays or downloads media itself. Known online thumbnails
may load separately as artwork.

## Optional projectM

Setup shows whether the Mac pack is installed. Choosing it opens the existing
confirmation for projectM 4, Cream of the Crop presets and MilkDrop textures;
it doesn't download until you confirm. Recent progress, the error and a retry
option are clearer. Music still works if installation fails, and a previous
working pack is kept. Linux retains its own companion/setup route.

Network/build/permission failures can still happen; installation requirements
are unchanged. No browser permissions or security settings change automatically.

## Update

```sh
brew update
brew upgrade jukeboxcli
jukeboxcli --version
```

Expect **1.8.0**, then quit and reopen. Source installs can use `./update.sh`.
Music, queues and preferences are kept. Physical remote-display/terminal and
long-session memory/GPU acceptance remain ongoing.
