# JukeboxCli 1.7.0

A more useful Home screen, plus an explicit way to play a public video when
browser cookies can't be read. One release for Mac and Linux.

## Home dashboard

Quick-launch cards open music search, YouTube videos, live radio, your local
library, the queue and optional downloads. The header shows current listening
and local/queue/station counts. Recent plays and saved stations have their own
panels, with queue actions on right-click. Arrows and Enter work throughout;
clicks require Mouse: click in Settings. Small windows keep a compact list.
The jukebox drawing stays. Opening Home never starts music or downloads.

## Retry without cookies

When cookie extraction fails, choose **Retry without cookies** on Home, click
the error-bar action, or press **m**, then **c** for player actions. This retries
the same queue item/mode/seekable position without cookie flags. It does not
change the saved browser/file choice or retry anonymously without asking.

The current item keeps that choice through audio/video switches. Next/new
selection and app restart return to configured cookies. Anonymous/configured
links have separate caches; the consent is never saved in a session. Downloads
keep their configured cookies. Restricted videos may still require authentication.

## Update

```sh
brew update
brew upgrade jukeboxcli
jukeboxcli --version
```

Expect **1.7.0**, then quit and reopen. Source installations can use `./update.sh`.
Music, queues and preferences are kept.

## Checks and limits

918 tests passed with 6 skipped on Apple Silicon macOS, including Home row/column
budgets, aligned cards, selection stability, mouse/keyboard actions and explicit
cookie retry/cache/session boundaries. Typecheck, build/import and production
redraw checks passed. Home documentation uses fictional isolated fixtures.

Real mpv acceptance uses isolated, muted playback and checks decoded audio, mode
switches, queue/position and owned-process cleanup. The cookie-retry check uses
a missing temporary profile rather than reading actual browser cookies.
Mac/Linux installation checks cover packaging and independent installs; Mac also
installs/tests the pinned Homebrew formula. Browser/YouTube restrictions, terminal
capabilities, physical devices and longer memory/GPU acceptance remain variable.
